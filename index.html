    How the numbers are calculated
    1. Productive hours = (shift length − breaks) × (1 − indirect %)
    2. Units per path = forecast × volume share %
    3. Labor hours = units ÷ planned rate
    4. HC needed = labor hours ÷ productive hours per associate
    5. HC to schedule = HC needed ÷ (1 − absenteeism %), rounded up
    6. Gap = available HC − HC to schedule
    7. Max shift output per path = available HC × show-up rate × productive hours × rate ÷ volume share. The lowest one is the bottleneck: the whole building can't ship more than that path can process.
